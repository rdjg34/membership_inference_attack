# Team Teamwork Contract

**Project:** COLX_531: TCOB Group Project

**Members:** Jennifer, Yucai, Christina, Rachelle 

---

We're here to do great work, learn, and support each other along the way. This contract serves to keep us organized, help us to communicate well, and ensure we're working well together.

---

## Communication
Our main hub for communication will be Slack. We ask that everyone does their best to respond to messages within 24 hours. If someone hasn't replied after that point, we may follow up with a call, or a quick in-person check-in.

## Meetings
The team will meet three times per week:

- **Tuesday morning** — Brief planning meeting to align on goals and distribute work for the coming week. Meeting time is 9 am. Attendance in-person or via slack call. 
- **Thursday afternoon** — In-person co-working session. Members who cannot attend in person may join online.


Brief minutes from Tuesday's meeting will be recorded and stored in the shared repository. These will be jointly edited in Slack and uploaded as a README file to the repo. 

## Work Distribution
Work will be distributed during the Tuesday planning meeting. The project manager is responsible for ensuring tasks are divided fairly and that all members are contributing equally.

## Project Manager
Each week, one of us takes on the project manager role. This person keeps the team on track, runs the Tuesday planning meeting, and ensures we meet our Friday deadline.

## Code Review
Code reviews will be completed on an as-needed basis and fully done by end of day Friday each week. The project manager is the code reviewer for their allocated week. (See table above).

## Quality of Work
We all want to be proud of what we produce! Team members will write clean, well-documented code and test before submitting for review. If a member is struggling with a task, they will raise it early.

Any unexpected unavailability should be flagged in Slack as early as possible.

## Code of Conduct
Above all, let's be kind and respectful to one another. A few things we commit to:

- Give feedback that is constructive and encouraging — we're learning!
- Speak up early if something isn't working, whether it's a technical problem or a team dynamic issue.
- MRI: Most Respectful Interpretation - assume the most positive, reasonable intent behind a team member's message.
- Do your best to meet deadlines, and communicate openly if something comes up.
- Celebrate each other's wins, big and small!
